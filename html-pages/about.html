GOLDEN BALL 2026

How it works.

The Golden Ball combines the passion of football fans with measurable player performance to decide a single winner.

THE FORMULA

More than just popularity.

Two inputs, one final score, out of 100.

01
👥

Fan Vote

30%

Every verified fan gets one vote for the candidate they believe deserves the Golden Ball. Your candidate's share of all fan votes cast becomes their Fan Vote Score.

02
📊

Season Stats

70%

Goals, assists, appearances, and major trophies are pulled automatically from a football data provider and run through a fixed scoring formula to produce a Stats Score.

03
🏆

Final Score

100%

Final Score = (Fan Vote Score × 0.30) + (Stats Score × 0.70). Whoever holds the highest Final Score when voting closes wins the Golden Ball.

STEP BY STEP

From vote to winner.

1
Fans vote once

Verified fans pick one candidate during the open voting window. No re-voting, no changing your pick after submitting.

2
Stats update automatically

Candidate performance data is refreshed from a football data provider so the Stats Score reflects real season form.

3
Scores are combined

Each candidate's Fan Vote Score and Stats Score are blended into one Final Score shown on the Leaderboard.

4
Highest score wins

Once voting closes, the candidate with the highest Final Score is named the Golden Ball winner.
